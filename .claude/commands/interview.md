Complete the task in $ARGUMENTS normally.

Then add a section titled **Interview Prep** with:

1. **Chosen approach**
   - Explain the solution as if defending it in a technical interview.
   - Focus on architecture, trade-offs, scalability, maintainability, and operational concerns.

2. **Alternative approach**
   - Present a realistic competing solution another engineer might prefer.
   - Explain when that approach would actually be better.

3. **Trade-off analysis**
   - Compare both approaches across (only if it is relevant,):
     - complexity
     - performance
     - readability
     - scalability
     - debugging
     - team ergonomics
     - operational risk

4. **Why this choice fits best here**
   - Justify the chosen solution specifically for this problem and constraints.
   - Avoid claiming universal superiority.

5. **How to validate the decision**
   - Suggest a benchmark, stress test, profiling method, production metric, or demo to prove the approach is appropriate.

IMPORTANT - try to use simple language that is optimized for understanding and remembering and not on deep dives
